2. Information we collect

To provide a personalized AI stylist experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and profile biometric metadata.
  • Visual content (“The Archive”): Images of clothing items and "Identity Proxy" face photos you upload.
  • Wardrobe metadata: AI-generated attributes such as category, color, material, and thermal grade.
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history handled securely via Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

3. How we use your information

  • Digitize and organize: Isolate garments onto white backgrounds for archival clarity.
  • Neural Draping: Synthesize images of your Identity Proxy wearing items from your Archive.
  • VR Simulation: Render your curated outfits in occasion-specific virtual environments.
  • Usage tracking: Deduct virtual credits based on the complexity of the AI task (HD vs Standard).

4. AI processing and third‑party services

Image analysis: Your images are processed by secure third‑party AI providers (Google Gemini/Vertex AI) to generate fashion metadata and renders. We do not sell your personal images to third‑party advertisers.

Cloud storage: We use encrypted cloud storage (Supabase/AWS) to ensure your Archive remains synchronized across your devices.

5. Data retention

  • Active accounts: We retain your Archive for as long as your account is active.
  • Permanent Deletion: Users may request permanent archival erasure via the "Danger Zone" in the Settings laboratory.
